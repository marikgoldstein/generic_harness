To owners, send logistics (launches, landings verbatim, resources, deadlines), general direction and needs (priorities, closing criteria, numbers first, prove before naming), and cross-item information clearly tagged as information. Do not tell an owner which route to take, which form to prove, or which input to staff first.

**Why:** once, in a running project, the lead sent every owner item-specific technical advice in one sweep. The co-author's reply: the owners hold the full picture of their item and decide technical strategy; the lead has the wider view and may inform them, and tells them logistics and general direction.

**How to apply:** relays of reviewer verdicts stay verbatim (that is information, not strategy). Ask owners what they choose and launch it. The lead still does mathematics directly when a short calculation or numerical check unblocks an item, handing the result to the owner as information (3.14).
