A paraphrased definition in a packet is the lead's claim about the record, and the prover proves or refutes the paraphrase.

**Why:** once, in a running project, outside provers refuted a statement twice, and both times the error was the lead's: a correction term's scope and a diagonal clause's quantifier had been paraphrased; the paraphrases were false while the record's sentences were true. A third time the record held two objects under one name and the packet did not say which.

**How to apply:** a writer at top effort copies the definitions from the source; a referee compares the packet with the source before it is sent; the gate also checks for zero hits of keys, paths, names and process words; the packet names the object when a name is overloaded.
