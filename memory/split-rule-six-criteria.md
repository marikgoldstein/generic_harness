Each open item gets one owner at top effort whose first task is to prove the item in full with the consumer line verbatim. It splits only if the item is far from a proof, and only if all six criteria hold: own statements and a fixed assembly written in advance; parts parallel now; each part separately checkable; no process steps as parts; the split follows the proof's structure; each part worth an agent or lane.

**Why:** once, in a running project, the lead split an item into many pieces, some forced: geometric cases, a part that was only a check, pieces added mid-way. The co-author questioned whether the split was natural and asked for the criteria.

**How to apply:** big items (far from a proof after the first full pass) keep a persistent owner plus LANES_PER_BIG_ITEM lanes; small items get LANES_PER_PIECE per open part and no persistent agent. The level reached is a floor until the item closes. Without lanes, the owner cycles distinct routes itself and records each. Parts are mathematics only.
