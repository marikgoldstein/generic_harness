Every status, report and plain-word line gives per item: pieces left x of y; named inputs left x of y, split into proved or of record, with a live route, and open mathematics. Never "basically done", "only bookkeeping", "just a few inputs", "digits only" or "one step short" while any input of the last two kinds remains; name the open-mathematics inputs first.

**Why:** once, in a running project, the lead told the user an item was "proved and reviewed except small inputs"; one of those inputs then turned out false as an identity and needed new work, and another item's single remaining input took most of a day. The user asked for transparent counts instead.

**How to apply:** the per-item field is a count, never a phrase; a "close ruling next" word carries the owner's named-input count in the same line. Flag inputs whose outcome is uncertain (route refuted, numerics only, deferred by the source) separately from routine ones. Place each reported piece in the item's remaining list ("step k of n left"; "this unlocks ..."), and give each followed item its ordered road to close at least hourly (6.8).
