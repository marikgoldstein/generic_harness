An outside prompt (a published problem statement, a question to another model) is written by a strong-model agent from a brief, never drafted by the lead; before it goes out, one independent check quotes the consumer's line and compares direction, sign, the orientation of every ratio and the units with the prompt's target.

**Why:** once, in a running project, a published prompt defined a ratio upside down and asked for the bound on the side no consumer used. An outside model proved exactly that side in five lines, the lead reported it as likely to close the item, and the owner caught the flip minutes later. The prompt had been internally consistent, so nothing looked wrong.

**How to apply:** when an outside answer makes an open step easy, confirm its orientation with the consumer before reporting progress; say "pending the direction check" until then. A verdict by one owner's prover on another owner's input (not needed, not consumed, refuted) is a proposal until the consumer's owner and its pair confirm. Freeze an outside prompt's content at a fixed time with an "as of" note; later results go as a separate addendum. Related: [[consumer-exact-object]], [[relay-verbatim-all-blocking-items]].
