Extracted text garbles formulas. When a display matters, a local agent reads the rendered page image; its reading is a claim; a second, blind reader confirms it glyph for glyph before any grade or digit changes on it.

**Why:** once, in a running project, printed displays cited by the record came out garbled in text extraction (indices and exponents merged), and a grade had been set from the garbled text. The two-reader rule caught readings that differed in one subscript.

**How to apply:** render the page at a legible resolution into the agent's own scratch directory; brief reader 1 and reader 2 separately with the same pages; compare their transcriptions character by character; a disagreement goes back to both with the page, never settled by the lead's own glance. A permanent agent's own image readings are claims under the same rule. Related: [[literature-sweep-before-not-printed]], [[outside-answers-claims-until-two-readers]].
