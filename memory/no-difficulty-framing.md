Describe open items neutrally: "the open inputs", "the items still to prove", "remaining". Two groups only: "close / almost done" and "other todos", each with the next step and an owner. New todos are reported in the same flat tone as closures.

**Why:** once, in a running project, the lead called some open inputs "the hard part"; the co-author answered that they were not really that hard and that the word predisposed everyone toward naming them as hypotheses instead of proving them. Later the user asked to drop "critical path", then "central", then "setbacks" (they are just todos), then "the harder ones" (nobody knew they were hard). Each word had slipped back in under a new form.

**How to apply:** when arguing that an input should be proved rather than named, say it carries the theorem's content, not that it is hard. Labels for item groups point toward finishing, not toward urgency or difficulty. Reviewer vocabulary like "blocking finding" stays inside the review files.
