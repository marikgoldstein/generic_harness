An onboarding v1 goes on disk by ONBOARDING_V1_AT with an addendum at every landing and a successor-state paragraph when the lead asks. The successor's brief names the onboarding (it governs) and the latest predecessor brief whole; older briefs only by their rules paragraphs; record lines by exact line or a short keyword window. The predecessor keeps working until the successor's "up", then forwards only; it writes no FINAL unless the lead names its successor.

**Why:** once, in a running project, an owner's session was closed by the harness before it wrote its successor's onboarding and the memo's resume section had to serve; a successor's brief chain cost a hundred and twenty-five thousand tokens of reads before any work; several seats reached their hand-over marks within forty minutes of spawning on briefs that measured three times their stated size. Four successions in one hour without an ownerless interval followed once the briefs were measured and the seat rule held.

**How to apply:** measure a brief's required reads in bytes before sending. A successor's first act tests the existence of every landing file the hand-over names, including the predecessor's last writes, and takes every pending landing. A stopped run is first resumed by one message; the successor is spawned when the resumed output degrades. Related: [[watch-agent-token-counts]], [[owner-persistence-digest]].
