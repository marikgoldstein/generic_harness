description: The writer of a text of record applies only the refuters' minors and adds nothing of its own; every number in a final text is a number a refuter confirmed (5.20)

A writer's job is mechanical: the prover's proof verbatim, the confirmed minors applied and listed (old / new), a `Lead decisions` section for anything that would change the statement, an input or a constant's dependence. A helpful aside is a defect.

**Why:** once, in a running project, a writer added three numerical parentheticals to a text of record (a bound "for all N", an equality case, a computed value), none of which any refuter had checked and all of which were false. The closure re-read caught them; without the re-read they would have been printed as confirmed.

**How to apply:** brief writers with the stop rule and the "nothing of your own" rule; have the closure re-read check the Changes section against the refuters' minors; treat any sentence in a final text that no lane confirmed as unconfirmed, whoever wrote it.
