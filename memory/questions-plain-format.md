Every question that passes the filter uses one shape, in plain natural language, short: what the plan or statement was; what we found (proved, refuted, numerics); what we do not know; the options, each saying whether the main result stays unconditional or becomes conditional; the recommended option with one reason; the default if no answer arrives, with the time you will act on it.

**Why:** once, in a running project, a question dense with symbols, internal labels and lane names was sent to a co-author who needed to see the decision at a glance from a phone. The user asked for "full natural language that is unmannered and easy to read", and the plain rewrite was answered within minutes.

**How to apply:** no symbols beyond the one number that matters; no internal lane, file or ruling names; agents by role. Under the channel's length cap. The same shape serves decision posts to the user (see [[decision-posts-three-lines]] for the shorter variant when the lead has already decided).
