When a lemma is open, say what you believe is true, why (numerics, structure, known theorems), and the expected sharp form. Then pose the weakest statement that suffices, and note the strongest the evidence supports.

**Why:** once, in a running project, the lead wrote "why I think (P) is true" with the numerics and the expected sharp exponent before the proof landed; the numerics showed a feared edge growth was absent and the open piece reduced to a standard estimate. The user named this a good method and asked for it to continue.

**How to apply:** every post on an open item opens with the belief, the evidence and the expected sharp form. Reduce step by step toward a named standard result with its exact citation, and say whether the reduction holds down to it. If the numerics contradict a heuristic, say so and re-target. Keep two words apart: the truth estimate ("TRUE with room 60x on the evidence") and the provable bound ("not provable by the a-priori chain at any feasible level"); the deliverable of record is chosen on the provable word.
